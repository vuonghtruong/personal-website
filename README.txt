JavaScript adds local publication search and an interactive two-clock model. The model supports pause/play, stops when out of view, and starts still when reduced motion is preferred. The figure represents dorsal (red) and ventral (blue) SCN neuronal populations with separate repulsive -k1 and attractive +k2 coupling. Effective relative-phase coupling is k2-k1, while mean-phase dynamics depend on k1+k2. Both phases are integrated using RK4. Red/blue cosines, their normalized mean, envelope E = |cos((thetaV-thetaD)/2)|, torus and phase dials are synchronized. Balanced nonzero interactions are distinguished from uncoupled clocks. This reduced illustration sets beta=0, fixes intrinsic periods and omits light forcing, the systemic oscillator and adaptation from the full source model. Parameters are illustrative, not fitted to experimental recordings.
